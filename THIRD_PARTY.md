# Third-party material

## Material Design Icons 7.4.47

The plant of the `bloom` style is six icons from
[Material Design Icons](https://pictogrammers.com) by Pictogrammers, as
published in `@mdi/svg` 7.4.47, under the Apache License 2.0
([LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt)). The package ships no
NOTICE file.

| File in `sprites/mdi/` | Stage of the plant | Nerd Font codepoint | Author |
|---|---|---|---|
| `seed.svg` | seed | U+F0E62 | Michael Irigoyen |
| `sprout.svg` | sprout | U+F0E66 | Michael Irigoyen |
| `leaf.svg` | leaf | U+F032A | Austin Andrews |
| `clover.svg` | clover | U+F0816 | Michael Richins |
| `palmate.svg` | palmate leaf | U+F07A6 | Austin Andrews |
| `spa.svg` | lotus | U+F0CD1 | Google |

Where the icons are and what is changed:

- `sprites/mdi/*.svg` are copies of the originals. `palmate.svg` is renamed,
  and the `id` attribute of its `<svg>` element is removed; its path is
  unchanged.
- `hooks/styles/bloom-sprites.ts` holds pictures of the icons, generated by
  `sprites/gen_sprites.py`. Each icon is cut to the square from (2, 2) to
  (22, 22) of its 24-unit view box, rasterised to a 36×36 pixel mask in a
  50×48 pixel picture, and filled with one pastel colour.
- Where the terminal's font has the icons, `bloom` draws them as text by
  their Nerd Font codepoints. The font is the terminal's own: the mod ships
  no icon font.

## JetBrains Mono 2.304

The glyph masks in `hooks/core/atlas.ts`, which the `holo` style draws its
word from, are rasterised from
[JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) 2.304
(`fonts/ttf/JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf` of the release), Copyright 2020 The
JetBrains Mono Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
([LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt](LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt)). The masks are under the same
licence.

Where the masks are and what is changed:

- `hooks/core/atlas.ts` holds one 8-bit alpha mask per character, generated
  by `font/gen_atlas.py`: each glyph drawn at 40 pixels in a 25×48 pixel
  cell, ink outside the cell cut off.
- The font file is not in the repository.
